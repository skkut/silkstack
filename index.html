ABOUT

Your generation history,
turned into a library

Every AI artist accumulates thousands of images — with their secrets hidden in the metadata. SilkStack Image Browser reads those secrets and turns your output folders into a searchable, taggable, beautifully rendered library. All offline, all on your machine.

🧠 Prompt · model · seed parsed
⚡ ~98% faster indexing
🔒 100% local — zero cloud

Built for the AI image workflow

SilkStack Image Browser scans your folders and parses rich metadata from ComfyUI, Automatic1111 and a dozen other tools — reading everything from the prompt and model to the sampler, CFG, seed and even the full workflow graph. What you get back is a library you can actually live in.

  • ◈ Deep metadata parsing: ComfyUI, A1111 / Forge / Fooocus / SD.Next, InvokeAI, SwarmUI, Midjourney, DALL·E 3, Firefly, DreamStudio, Draw Things
  • ◈ Automatic folder watching — new generations appear live while you wait
  • ◈ Search by prompt text, model, steps, CFG, sampler, seed — plus portrait / landscape / square filters
  • ◈ Drag an image straight back into ComfyUI to reload its prompt and workflow

FEATURES

Everything your library
has been missing

Hover the modules. They feel the way they should.

Universal Metadata

Parses prompt, model, steps, CFG, sampler, seed and workflows from ComfyUI, A1111, InvokeAI, Midjourney, DALL·E 3, Firefly and more — even from WebP.

11+ TOOLS

Adaptive Grid

A beautiful image grid that auto-adjusts to every image's aspect ratio, with buttery smooth scrolling — even when your folder holds 100,000 generations.

SMOOTH-SCROLL

Auto-Watch

Point it at your output folders and watch new images appear in real time, the moment your sampler finishes. No refresh, no rescan, no waiting.

LIVE-INDEX

Search & Filters

Find anything by prompt text, model, steps, CFG, sampler or seed. Filter by portrait, landscape or square. Your full history, queried in a keystroke.

CTRL+K

Drag & Drop to ComfyUI

Drag any image back into ComfyUI to instantly reload its prompt and workflow graph. Works with Photoshop, Discord and your file explorer too.

DND-READY

Tags & Collections

Auto-tags plus your own manual tags, clustered smart stacks, collections, folders with emoji icons, and removable drives — including encrypted ones.
