AI INTELLIGENCE

Local AI that reads
what you meant

Semantic search, automatic tagging and similarity stacks — run by small AI models on your own GPU through WebGPU. No cloud service, no account, no API keys, and your images, prompts and tags are never uploaded anywhere.

silkstack://ai-intelligence ON-DEVICE

Semantic Search

Keyword search matches words; semantic search matches meaning. Describe an image loosely — “a girl with a puppy in a palace” — and SilkStack finds generations whose prompts mean the same thing, even when they share almost no words, and even in another language.

MEANING, NOT WORDS

AI Auto-Tagging

A small local LLM reads the prompt inside every image and writes up to 15 searchable tags — subjects, styles, lighting, concepts — including synonyms and alternate phrasings a searcher might type. Tags sit alongside your manual ones, and an interrupted run resumes where it stopped.

UP TO 15 TAGS / IMAGE

Similarity Stacks

Prompts are embedded with the same local model that powers search, so reworded, translated and synonym-only variations land in the same stack. Group by Prompt, Model or Loras, and highlight exactly which words changed between two generations.

VECTOR GROUPING
✦ Differing words highlighted
◈ One stack, 40 variations

Spot what changed
between generations

A folder holding forty variations of the same idea becomes a few tidy cards. Vector similarity is what makes the grouping smart: prompts are compared by what they mean, not just how they are worded, so “a girl in a pink gown” and “a princess wearing a pink dress” end up in the same stack.

  • ◈ Group by Prompt, Model or Loras — or turn grouping off entirely
  • ◈ Highlight marks the exact words that differ between the prompts in a stack — spot an added colour, an age, a swapped adjective at a glance
  • ◈ Vector matching can only add images to a stack — it never breaks up a group that text matching already found
  • ◈ Keep working without AI: plain text matching still stacks your library, and the browser itself never needs a model

Your GPU,
your rules

Local AI is only worth it if you stay in control of it. SilkStack shows exactly which models are loaded, how much VRAM they hold, how much disk they take — and gives you a switch for all of it.

  • ◈ One top-bar toggle turns off model loading, semantic search and auto-tagging together, so the app stays out of your GPU while you generate
  • ◈ The footer model pill shows every model resident in GPU memory with its declared VRAM, plus an Eject button to hand the memory straight back
  • ◈ Settings → AI Intelligence lists each cached model with its size and deletes them one by one to reclaim disk space
  • ◈ Models are downloaded once and then run fully offline; on a dual-GPU laptop you pick which GPU they use
